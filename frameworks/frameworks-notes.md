# Theoretical Frameworks - Working Notes

Candidate frameworks and their role in this thesis:

| Framework | Source | Role here | Limitation for GenAI |
|---|---|---|---|
| UTAUT | Venkatesh et al. 2003 | Structural backbone (PE, EE, SI, FC -> intention) | No trust or risk constructs; assumes deterministic tech |
| TrAAIT | Stevens & Stetson 2023 | Trust antecedents (credibility, value, reliability) and trust -> acceptance path | Single-site; not generative-specific |
| Trust calibration | Asan et al. 2020; Lee & See 2004 | Defines the dependent concept: calibrated, not maximal, trust | Conceptual; needs operationalisation |
| Multi-level acceptance factors | Lambert et al. 2023 | Individual / technological / organisational layering for RQ3 | Pre-generative evidence base |
| XAI scepticism | Ghassemi et al. 2021 | Null/negative hypothesis for RQ2 | Predictive-model paradigm |

Working synthesis: UTAUT core + TrAAIT trust mediation + explainability-form antecedents + organisational moderators. Diagram to follow after supervisor discussion.
